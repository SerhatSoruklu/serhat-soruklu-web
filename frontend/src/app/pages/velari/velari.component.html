The framework

What Is Velari?

Velari is a modern spiritual and philosophical belief framework founded by Serhat Soruklu. It combines a symbolic reverence for Light and the Sun with pantheistic ideas about the divine presence within existence, while placing discipline, compassion, wisdom and personal responsibility at the centre of daily life.

It is structured as a framework rather than a demand for unquestioning belief. Its principles are intended to be studied, practised, discussed and refined without treating any living individual as divine or infallible.

Many paths, one Light.

Coexistence without erasing difference.

The symbol of Light

The Light Within All Things

In Velari, the Sun is a sacred symbol of the life-giving Light rather than a human-like god. Light represents truth, wisdom, awareness and the capacity to transform. Darkness represents confusion or ignorance—not people who are inherently evil.

Helio-pantheism is Velari’s synthesis of solar symbolism and the belief that sacred presence can be encountered throughout existence. The physical Sun offers a visible image of life, clarity and energy; it is not presented as scientific proof of a theology.

Choice and consequence

Freedom Carries Responsibility

Velari treats free will as meaningful choice, determination as the strength to continue, and karma as consequence, character formation and reciprocal effect. Choices shape the chooser, relationships and the wider world.

Karma is not a licence to make easy judgments about another person’s suffering or to excuse the person who caused it.

Required boundary

Velari does not teach that victims deserve harm because of karma. Responsibility belongs to those who choose harmful actions.

Many paths

Respect Without Erasure

Velari does not require hostility toward other religions or philosophies. It can study wisdom across traditions while retaining its own identity and principles.

“Many paths, one Light” expresses coexistence and shared dignity. It does not claim that every tradition is identical.

The long future

Built to Outlive Its First Form

Velari is designed for the long horizon. Its principles and texts may be studied, questioned and refined by future generations while its central commitments to Light, discipline, compassion, truth and responsibility remain visible.

No prediction can determine how widely Velari will one day be followed. Its future depends on whether people continue to find honest value in its principles.
