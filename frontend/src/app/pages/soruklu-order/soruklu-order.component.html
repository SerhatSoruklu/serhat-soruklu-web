I · The Order

A Selective Family Initiative

The Soruklu Order is a small, selective family initiative established in 2024 and led by Serhat Soruklu. It brings together approximately five to ten members around shared principles, voluntary responsibilities, and long-term family continuity.

Membership is selective and voluntary. The Order exists to support continuity, responsibility, cooperation, and careful record-keeping among its selected members.

Internal titles organize responsibilities within the initiative. They do not grant public office, legal authority, or control over any person outside it.

II

Identity boundary

The Order Is Not the Entire Family

The wider Soruklu family is a large and diverse extended family spanning many independent households and potentially thousands of individuals. The Soruklu Order is a separate, selective initiative consisting of approximately five to ten members. It does not represent every person with the Soruklu surname, and membership in the wider family does not imply membership in, endorsement of, or involvement with the Order.

The wider family is not centrally controlled and is not collectively represented by this initiative.

VI · Lawful conduct

Safeguarding, Evidence and Due Process

The Soruklu Order supports the lawful reporting and investigation of sexual abuse, violence, exploitation, and other serious wrongdoing. It supports safeguarding vulnerable people, preserving credible evidence, due process, the presumption of innocence, and meaningful support for victims and survivors.

These principles apply universally. They must not be misused to make collective allegations against an entire family, surname, or group of unrelated households.
