CODARIS

Coalition Of Developers Advancing Responsible Intelligent Systems

An independent, developer-focused coalition creating practical work around responsible intelligent systems: careful verification, security, safety, interoperability, and clear evidence about what systems do.

PURPOSE

From shared questions to useful engineering

CODARIS describes a direction for developer collaboration. Its structure should grow through useful, reviewable work.
